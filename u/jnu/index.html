What a JnU cover page needs

Jagannath University assignments are handed in behind a cover sheet carrying the university name and crest, the assignment title, the course title and code, the teacher's name, designation and department, and the student's own name, ID, section and session. covergen lays all of that out for you and keeps every field editable.

Frequently asked questions

Is the JnU assignment cover page generator free?

Yes. covergen is free for every JnU student, with no account and no limit on how many covers you make. It runs entirely in your browser, so your name, student ID and teacher's name never leave your device.

What goes on a JnU assignment cover page?

A Jagannath University cover page carries the university name and crest, the assignment title, the course title and code, your teacher's name and department under Submitted To, and your name, ID, section and session under Submitted By.

Does it have JnU departments and teachers?

Yes. JnU ships with 38 departments across 7 faculties and 657 teachers. Search a teacher's name and their designation and department fill in automatically. Anything missing can be typed by hand.

How do I save the JnU cover page as a PDF?

Press Print and choose Save as PDF as the destination. The cover prints as vector at exactly 210 by 297 millimetres, so the text stays selectable and sharp instead of coming out as a blurry screenshot.

Can I join the cover to my finished JnU assignment?

Yes. Choose Merge assignment and pick your assignment PDF. You get back a single file with the cover as page one and your own pages after it, ready to upload or print.

Is this an official university tool?

No. covergen is made by a student and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Jagannath University. The crest belongs to the university and is included only so your printed cover matches what your department expects.
