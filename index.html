Common questions

Is covergen free?

Yes, completely, with no account and no limit on how many covers you make. It runs in your browser, so your name and ID never leave your device.

How do I save it as a PDF?

Press Print, then choose Save as PDF as the destination. The text stays selectable and sharp because it prints as vector, not as a screenshot.

Can I put the cover on my finished assignment?

Yes. Choose Merge assignment, pick your assignment PDF, and you get one file with the cover first and your pages after it.

Is this an official university tool?

No. covergen is made by a student and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any university. Each crest belongs to its university and is shown only so your printed cover matches what your department expects.

My teacher is not in the list. What now?

Type the name straight into the Teacher field. The directory is a shortcut, never a restriction, and every field stays editable.
